Alberto Hernández Marcos completed his PhD research in Information and Communication Technologies at the University of Granada, supervised by Eduardo Ros in the CITIC-UGR and Department of Computer Engineering, Automation and Robotics (ICAR) environment. His work explores reinforcement learning and self-learning emotional frameworks for artificial intelligence.

He is now Head of the GenAI Lab at BBVA AI Factory.
