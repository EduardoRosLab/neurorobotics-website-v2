abstract: "This work presents a methodology for creating a dataset to learn the dynamics of a collaborative robot. It fine-tunes proportional-derivative controllers across benchmark trajectories through multi-objective evolutionary algorithms that balance accuracy and low torque for safe human–robot interaction. The resulting data-driven inverse dynamic model is validated in a feedforward control loop and outperforms the individually tuned standard controllers."
