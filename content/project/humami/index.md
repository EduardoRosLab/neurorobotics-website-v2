HumAMI aims to strengthen the European AI ecosystem by establishing a robust Physical AI framework for machines, robots, and human-centric cyber-physical systems. It brings together edge AI, agentic AI, cloud resources, connectivity, and applications and services to support safe, explainable, trustworthy, ethical, and sustainable AI systems.

The framework develops innovative disruptive technologies, integrates them into combined solutions, and validates them in real-world demonstrators across mobility, energy, digital industry, health, agrifood, and digital society. The project is funded by the European Union through the HORIZON-JU-Chips-2025-RIA-two-stage call (grant 101252558).
