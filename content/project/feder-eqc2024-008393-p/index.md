The main objective of this national project is the **supply of scientific and technical industrial equipment for UGR-DONES** and for the **Unit of Mechatronics and Electronic Systems** at the University of Granada's Scientific Instrumentation Centre (CIC).

Total funding awarded for this infrastructure amounts to **€1,567,053.91**. The project began on **25 March 2025** and strengthens the mechatronics and robotics capabilities that support the **IFMIF-DONES particle accelerator**.

**Principal investigator:** Eduardo Ros  
**Researchers:** Niceto R. Luque and Francisco Barranco
