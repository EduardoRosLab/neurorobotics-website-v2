New publication from our group out in **Cell**.

<!--more-->

Our latest work presents a deep learning strategy to identify cell types across species from high-density extracellular recordings. This study advances our understanding of neural circuit organisation and offers a powerful computational tool for neuroscientists working with large-scale electrophysiology data.

You can find the full paper in our [publications section](/publication/naveros2025). Congratulations to all involved!
